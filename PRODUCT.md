# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users
Disciplined swing traders looking for a focused, noise-free environment to track their edge.

## Product Purpose
A setup-tagged trading journal and synchronized multi-chart control app, designed to help disciplined swing traders analyze and review their edge.

## Positioning
Your edge is found in disciplined journaling and synchronized chart review.

## Brand Personality
Disciplined, tactile, focused, and calm (like a physical field notebook).

## Anti-references
- TradingView's dark blue-on-black terminal look
- Generic AI-default looks (cream+terracotta, neon on black, zero-radius broadsheet)

## Design Principles
1. **Focus over Flash:** The tool serves the process. Avoid distractions that break the field notebook tone.
2. **Synchronized Control:** Multi-chart navigation is the core value proposition; it should feel effortless and tactile.
3. **Intentional Sparseness:** Keep the layout ledger-like. Use hairlines and restraint rather than heavy borders or deep shadows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Ensure high contrast for data legibility. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all transitions (crucially the global sync moment), falling back to instant or simple opacity transitions.
